# Example Report: Habit Tracker App

## Decision

Should an indie developer build a habit tracker app?

## Executive Takeaway

- Verdict: Watchlist unless the niche is very specific.
- Best wedge: Habit tracker for people managing recurring health routines with caregiver accountability.
- Why now: General habit tracking is saturated, but accountability and health-adjacent routines create higher stakes.
- Biggest risk: Users install habit apps aspirationally and churn quickly.
- Recommended next step: Test a simple shared routine check-in workflow.

## Category Snapshot

- Category: Habit tracking, routines, accountability.
- Target users: Self-improvement users, ADHD users, fitness users, health routine users.
- Jobs to be done: Remember routines, track streaks, feel accountable, review progress.
- Monetization patterns: Freemium, subscription, lifetime unlock.

## Competitor Matrix

| Product | Platform | Positioning | Monetization | Strength | Weakness / Complaint | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Streaks | iOS | Simple habit streaks | Paid app | Polished, focused | General purpose | Health/caregiver workflow |
| Habitify | iOS/Android/Web | Cross-platform habit tracking | Subscription | Broad platform support | Can feel complex | Opinionated niche setup |
| Done | iOS | Simple goal tracking | Freemium | Clean UI | General category | Accountability layer |
| Productive | iOS/Android | Routine and habit planner | Subscription | Strong onboarding | Subscription complaints | Lower-cost focused alternative |

## Pain Patterns

1. Users want accountability, not just streak counters.
2. Setup complexity kills activation.
3. General habit apps rarely match specific health or caregiving routines.

## Opportunity Ideas

| Idea | User Wedge | Score | Why It Could Work | Main Risk |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Shared health routine tracker | Caregivers and patients | 73 | Higher-stakes recurring workflow | Must avoid medical claims |
| ADHD-friendly micro habit app | ADHD users | 69 | Clear pain and community | Crowded, sensitive positioning |
| Couple accountability tracker | Couples | 61 | Social retention | Monetization unclear |

## Recommended MVP

- One-line concept: A shared checklist for recurring health routines, with gentle accountability and weekly summaries.
- Must-have features: Shared routine, check-in reminder, missed-task note, weekly summary.
- Deliberately excluded: Medical advice, complex analytics, public social features.
- Pricing hypothesis: Free for one shared routine, $3.99/month for multiple routines and history.
- Distribution wedge: Caregiver communities and SEO around medication/routine checklists.

## Final Recommendation

Validate only with a specific user segment. A generic habit tracker is too crowded for a solo developer.

# Report Methodology

## Analysis Order

1. Define the user's builder constraints.
2. Map the category and sub-niches.
3. Collect competitors and evidence.
4. Extract repeated pain patterns.
5. Identify underserved wedges.
6. Score opportunities using the rubric.
7. Recommend one path and one validation test.

## Competitor Matrix Columns

- Product
- Platform
- Positioning
- Monetization
- Strength
- Weakness or complaint pattern
- Opportunity gap

## Pain Pattern Extraction

Group raw complaints into themes:

- Price/value mismatch
- Missing workflow
- Bad UX/onboarding
- Poor AI quality
- Privacy/trust issue
- Export/import limitation
- Localization issue
- Platform limitation
- Slow support or abandoned product

## Handling Weak Evidence

If evidence is thin:

- Say so plainly.
- Lower confidence and score.
- Recommend a validation step instead of a build.
- Avoid invented market size estimates.

## Output Tone

Be direct and useful. The reader is deciding what to build, so prioritize tradeoffs, next actions, and reasons not to build.

# From-Scratch Idea Discovery Method

Use this method when the user does not have an app idea or category.

## Default Assumptions

- Solo indie developer.
- 1-2 week MVP.
- iOS or web app.
- English-speaking market first.
- Low budget and no paid data subscriptions.

## Signal Buckets

Scan at least 4 buckets before recommending ideas:

| Bucket | What To Look For | Example Searches |
|---|---|---|
| App store rankings/search | High ratings, visible IAP, crowded categories, weak positioning | "site:apps.apple.com AI notes", "site:play.google.com habit tracker" |
| Review pain | Repeated complaints in paid or popular apps | "app name reviews export expensive subscription" |
| Product launches | New products that signal active demand | Product Hunt, launch pages, changelogs |
| Community pain | Repeated "I wish", "alternative to", "too expensive", "how do I" posts | Reddit, HN, Indie Hackers |
| Pricing pages | Clear paid willingness | Official pricing pages, in-app purchase listings |
| GitHub/open source | Developer pain, stars on utilities, abandoned tools | GitHub search, issues, discussions |
| Search/video trends | Tutorial demand and consumer pull | Google Trends, YouTube/TikTok search results |

## Candidate Requirements

Each raw candidate should include:

- User segment.
- Problem trigger.
- Existing workaround or competitor.
- Monetization hint.
- Possible distribution channel.
- Why a solo developer can build the first version.

## Early Rejection Rules

Reject ideas if:

- The first version depends on a large proprietary dataset.
- The idea needs marketplace liquidity.
- Trust, medical, legal, or financial risk is too high for a lightweight MVP.
- Incumbents are free, polished, and hard to differentiate from.
- The idea is only "add AI to X" without a workflow wedge.

## Shortlisting Rules

Prefer ideas that have:

- Repeated pain across at least two sources.
- Competitors with paid plans or IAP.
- A specific niche wedge.
- A feasible MVP under two weeks.
- A non-viral distribution path.
